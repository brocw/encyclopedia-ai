A reader has asked for an encyclopedia article on: '%s'

Before anything is written, decide what that article is about.

- Give the article its encyclopedic title. Fix spelling and capitalization, and expand an abbreviation when the expansion is the conventional title.
- Say in one sentence what the subject is, the way a lead sentence would.
- Name the kind of subject in a word or two (person, place, organism, event, concept, technology, work, organization, and so on).
- List what a reference article on this subject must cover. These are areas of coverage, not section headings.
- If the same words could reasonably name something else, list the readings you set aside.
- List anything a reader might expect to find here that properly belongs in a different article.

Respond with ONLY valid JSON in this exact format:
{
  "title": "...",
  "subject": "one sentence",
  "kind": "...",
  "scope": ["area of coverage", "area of coverage"],
  "exclusions": ["what belongs in another article"],
  "ambiguities": ["reading of the topic that was not chosen"]
}

Give 3 to 8 scope entries. Use an empty array for any field that does not apply.
